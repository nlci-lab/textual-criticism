# Textual criticism field guide

A reference for Bible translators who meet the sigla of the UBS Greek New Testament apparatus in Paratext and need to know, quickly, what each witness is and why it matters.

## Witnesses

**Witness**:
Any source that gives evidence for the wording of the text: a Greek manuscript, a group of manuscripts, an ancient version, or a church father.
_Avoid_: Source (too broad), document, text

**Greek manuscript**:
A single handwritten copy of (part of) the New Testament in Greek, identified by its Gregory–Aland number.
_Avoid_: Codex (only one format), MS when the kind is unclear

**Papyrus / Majuscule / Minuscule / Lectionary**:
The four INTF categories of Greek manuscript. Papyrus is defined by material; majuscule and minuscule by script; lectionary by arrangement into church readings.
_Avoid_: Uncial (use majuscule; keep "uncial" only when quoting older literature)

**Family**:
A group of manuscripts that share a common ancestor closely enough to be cited as one witness (ƒ¹, ƒ¹³).
_Avoid_: Group, cluster, text-type

**Group reading**:
A witness that stands for the agreement of many manuscripts rather than one: `Byz`, `Lect`, and their part-readings `Byzᵖᵗ`, `Lectᵖᵗ`.
_Avoid_: Majority Text (that is a different edition)

**Version**:
An ancient translation of the New Testament (Old Latin, Vulgate, Syriac, Coptic, and others), cited as a witness to the Greek it was translated from.
_Avoid_: Translation (reserve for modern translations), recension

**Version manuscript**:
One identified manuscript or edition within a version, shown by a superscript (`itᵃ`, `syrˢ`, `copˢᵃ`).
_Avoid_: Sub-version

**Father**:
A church writer whose quotations of the New Testament are cited as a witness.
_Avoid_: Patristic source, author

**Modern edition**:
A printed edition or modern translation cited in the discourse segmentation apparatus (WH, TR, NA, NRSV…). Not a witness.
_Avoid_: Witness (for these)

## Naming a witness

**Siglum** (plural **sigla**):
The short symbol a witness carries in the UBS apparatus, written exactly as printed (`א`, `B`, `𝔓⁶⁶`, `ƒ¹³`, `itᵃ`).
_Avoid_: Abbreviation, code, symbol (for a witness)

**GA number**:
The Gregory–Aland number INTF assigns to a Greek manuscript (01, 03, P66, 1739, L2211).
_Avoid_: Gregory number, catalogue number

**Alias**:
Any other string a reader might type to mean a witness: a Latin transliteration, a GA number with or without leading zero, a name.
_Avoid_: Synonym, nickname

**Modifier**:
A mark attached to a siglum that narrows which part or hand of the witness is cited (`*`, `¹`, `ᶜ`, `ᵛⁱᵈ`, `ᵐᵍ`, `ˢᵘᵖᵖ`, `ᵖᵗ`, `²ᐟ⁴`). A siglum plus modifiers still points to the same witness.
_Avoid_: Suffix, flag

**Corrector**:
A later hand that changed a manuscript's text, cited with a numbered or `c` modifier (`א²`, `Bᶜ`). A corrector belongs to its manuscript; it is never a witness of its own.
_Avoid_: Correction (the act), editor

**Original hand**:
The first scribe's reading, marked `*`.
_Avoid_: First hand (acceptable in prose, not as a label)

## Reading the apparatus

**Variation unit**:
A place in the text where witnesses differ, given one entry in the apparatus.
_Avoid_: Variant (that is one reading), textual problem

**Reading**:
One of the forms of the text at a variation unit, with the witnesses that support it.
_Avoid_: Variant reading (only when contrasting with the printed text)

**Rating**:
The letter {A}–{D} the UBS committee gives a variation unit to show how certain it was about the reading printed in the text.
_Avoid_: Grade, confidence score

**Key variation unit**:
A variation unit where a particular witness is decisive or famous, listed on that witness's profile under "Where it matters".

## Evidence about the evidence

**Profile**:
The page that describes one witness.
_Avoid_: Entry, article

**Verified / Unverified**:
A fact is verified when a named, checkable source supports it; otherwise it is unverified and is shown separately, never mixed with verified facts.

**Finding aid**:
A source used only to find leads (Wikipedia, unsourced sigla lists). Nothing from a finding aid is shown as fact until a cited source confirms it.

**UBS5 list check**:
Whether a witness has been confirmed against the printed UBS5 list of witnesses (Introduction, pp. 10*ff.). Separate from whether its facts are verified.
