James 2:20: ἀργή or νεκρά?

Faith without works is “useless” in some Bibles and “dead” in others. Look at the entry and decide which reading came first.

ReadingSome witnessesEarliest
ἀργή “useless”B, C*, 1739, some Latin, Sahidic Coptic4th c.
νεκρά “dead”א, A, C², Byz, Syriac4th c.
κενή “empty”𝔓⁷⁴, one Latin manuscript7th c.

Question

The external evidence is close. Which clue best explains how “dead” could arise from “useless”?
