# Unchecked claims: inventory before launch

Snapshot of 20 Nov 2026, taken from the built site (`dist/`) and the witness files. Everything here is already
marked on the site ("not yet checked", "to check", "Not yet verified", or the page banner), so readers are
warned; this list is for deciding what to **check**, **reword**, or **cut** before the under-construction banner
and `noindex` come off.

Legend: **Check** = find the page in the named source and confirm or correct. **Cut?** = could simply be
removed if no source is found. Book sources marked "not yet checked" are ones we cited from memory or second-hand.

## 1. Most-used unchecked source (fixes many items at once)

**Metzger & Ehrman, *The Text of the New Testament*, 4th ed. (2005)** is cited, unchecked, on: Vaticanus, Sinaiticus,
Bezae, 𝔓⁶⁶, Byz, materials, nt-text, base-texts, John 7:53–8:11. Checking it against a copy would clear most of
section 3 below.

Other unchecked books: Parker, *Introduction to the NT Manuscripts* (materials, nt-text); Parker, *Codex Bezae*
(Bezae); Hurtado, *Earliest Christian Artifacts* (materials); Aland & Aland, *Text of the NT* (nt-text, glossary
"Aland category"); Wasserman & Gurry, *A New Approach to Textual Criticism* (nt-text, weighing-evidence); Tov,
Würthwein–Fischer and the BHS introduction (ot-text); Metzger, *Textual Commentary* on Romans 16:7 (heard read aloud
in the Hemphill talk, book not checked).
